What is free now, and what the paid plan will cover

2026-09-09

Right now every MU:note feature is free. A few of them are beta features, so we want to say this in advance.

  • D-day and to-do — Open as beta features for now. On the day the paid plan opens they become paid features. Anything you have already written stays visible and is not deleted. Adding or editing will be part of the paid plan.
  • Practice records, reflection memos and the calendar — Stay free. Details of sessions older than 3 months (pieces, memos, MU:note's note) will be part of the paid plan. Calendar shading and monthly totals stay free forever.
  • MU:note's note — Currently a beta feature, free 10 times a month. When the paid plan opens it becomes unlimited. The free plan keeps its 10 a month.

Thank you to everyone around the world who is using MU:note.
Any opinion or feedback is welcome.
Thank you.
