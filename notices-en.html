D-day and to-do will become paid-plan features

2026-09-08

Right now every MU:note feature is free while we are in beta. Here is something we want you to know in advance.

  • D-day and to-do will become paid-plan features on the day the paid plan opens. Anything you have already written stays visible and is not deleted. Adding and editing from that day on will be part of the paid plan.
  • If you want to add, edit and delete freely at any time, use memos. Memos stay free.
  • Practice records, reflection memos, the calendar and monthly totals stay free. Once the paid plan opens, the details of sessions older than 3 months (pieces, memos, MU:note's note) will be part of the paid plan. Calendar shading and monthly totals stay free forever.
  • We will announce the price and the start date here first, as soon as they are decided.

We are telling you early for one reason: nothing you use for free should be locked without notice.

New notices are posted on this page first.
