Pick a Muni that plays your instrument

2026-09-15

Until now the practice room had only four Munis. If you play cello, yours held a violin; if you play bassoon, yours held a flute. Thank you to everyone who told us.

From this update you can choose your Muni.

How to choose. After the update, a card appears once on the home screen. The same picker opens from Main instrument in Settings. If your instrument isn't listed, pick the closest Muni.

Your instrument can be set only once. A Muni can't be changed after you pick it — neither the instrument family nor the drawing. We ask you to confirm before it's set. If you ever need a change, tell us through Settings → Contact us. We'll help.

Instrument and Muni are two different things. Your instrument sets how sound is judged: it is one of piano, strings, winds, voice, or guitar, and MU:note listens to your practice by that family. Your Muni is how you appear in the practice room. Switching to the cello Muni doesn't change how your practice is heard.
