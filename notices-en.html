What is free now, and what the paid plan will cover

2026-09-11

Right now every MU:note feature is free. A few of them are beta features, so we want to say this in advance.

  • D-day and to-do — Open as beta features for now. On the day the paid plan opens they become paid features. Anything you have already written stays visible and is not deleted. Adding or editing will be part of the paid plan.
  • If you want to add, edit and delete freely at any time, use the memo during practice. Memos stay free.
  • Practice records, reflection memos and the calendar — Free forever.
  • MU:note's note — Currently a beta feature, free once a day and up to 10 times a month. When the paid plan opens it becomes unlimited. The free plan keeps offering it within the planned limit.
  • We will announce the price and the start date here first, as soon as they are decided.

A few more people find MU:note every day. Thank you for keeping it beside your practice.
If anything feels off or you wish for something, tell us anytime via Contact us in Settings. Even a small note makes the next update better.
Thank you.
