MUSU ("we") operates MU:note, an automatic practice-tracking app for musicians ("Service"). We collect as little as possible — the app only records what it observes about your practice, nothing more.

Service operation records exist so we can find the cause when something goes wrong — for example, "I practiced but nothing was recorded." Piece names, notes, and reflections you type are never included, and both the app and the server filter them out mechanically. These records are deleted automatically after 90 days, and they are removed along with everything else if you delete your account, regardless of that period.

Guest (not signed-up) practice records are also stored on our server for syncing. They are not linked to an account, so access is lost once the app is deleted; such orphaned records will be cleaned up periodically.

Friends only starts when a signed-in member sets a name in the Friends tab and exchanges a friend code directly. We never read your contacts or address book. Friends can always see your name and main instrument; whether you practiced today, whether you are practicing now, today's total practice time, your streak, practice days this week, and focus are shown only if you turn each one on. Piece names, notes, reflections, today's practice goals, D-days, and daily notes are never shown to friends under any circumstances. These numbers are not stored separately — they are calculated from your practice records each time a friend looks. Turn an item off and it disappears from the next refresh.

What we never collect: the app uses your microphone to detect practice sounds, but raw audio is never stored or transmitted under any circumstances. Sound is classified on your device ("is this an instrument?") and immediately discarded. We do not access your location, contacts, or photos.

2. Why we use it

  • Saving, syncing, and restoring your practice records (log in after reinstalling to get them back)
  • Practice statistics, calendar, and encouragement notes ("a note from MU:note")
  • Account management, age verification (14+), and support
  • Providing the Friends feature (connecting friends, today's cards and reactions, the practicing-now indicator), handling reports and preventing misuse

When generating encouragement notes, only summarized practice statistics (such as practice time, weekday, and time-of-day patterns), practice types, and your app language are sent for AI processing — your typed notes and piece names are never sent. This only happens when generating notes for signed-in members. Nothing is sent for AI processing while you use the app without an account, and the first welcome note is created on your device without any external transfer.

4. Retention and deletion

  • All your data is deleted immediately and permanently from our servers when you delete your account.
  • Service operation records are deleted automatically after 90 days, even while your account stays active.
  • Practice records you delete in the app are removed within 30 days; deleted practice goals, D-days and daily notes have their content erased immediately on our servers. Only a content-free identifier remains so the item cannot reappear on another device.
  • Friends data is cleaned up as follows: reactions you send are deleted after 30 days, and records of declined friend requests after 7 days. The practicing-now signal disappears 10 minutes after the last signal and is deleted immediately when you turn that item off. Your block list is kept until you unblock. Reports are kept for one year after they are handled, then deleted.
  • In case a failure damages the data, we keep an encrypted backup copy on the operator's administrative device for 30 days; backups older than 30 days are deleted automatically. When you delete your account, the data is removed from the server immediately and disappears from the backups within 30 days at the latest.
  • You can delete your account inside the app: Settings → Delete account. Deletion cannot be undone.

5. Your rights

You can view your data (in the app), edit it (in the app), delete it (account deletion), and contact us below with any privacy request.

6. Children

The Service is not available to children under 14. Sign-up requires confirming you are 14 or older.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), per-account access control (you can only access your own data), and the principle of least privilege. Friends data is shared only with people who are confirmed friends, and only through server-defined procedures — the app cannot read another person's records directly.
